Core Application Modules

• Portfolio Architecture Setup
This component processes an array of distinct financial assets, treating them as a unified portfolio rather than isolated stocks. It handles variable inputs like user-defined Ticker Symbols (e.g., AAPL, TSLA, SPY) and dynamic Portfolio Weights that must sum to 100%, allowing users to customize and test unique asset allocation strategies.

• Capital Injection Schedule
This feature models non-linear cash flow distributions over time to move beyond static, lump-sum calculations. It accepts completely custom user parameters for One-time Purchases (discretionary execution dates and capital amounts) and toggleable Recurring Investments, simulating the exact wealth-compounding impact of Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA).

• Temporal Framing Engine
This module controls the specific historical baseline used to calibrate the mathematical projections. It allows users to modify the Historical Period Bounds (selecting custom 'From' and 'To' calendar dates), giving them the flexibility to test how their portfolio setup survives across different historical market cycles.

• Data Ingestion Layer
Serving as the system's empirical foundation, this pipeline cleans and sanitizes raw market microstructure information for the engine. It supports multiple user-selected Data Sourcing Methods, letting the user toggle between streaming live, split-adjusted data from the Yahoo Finance API, uploading a custom local CSV file, or loading sample sets.

• Stochastic Forecast Simulator
The application's mathematical core generates a fan chart of 100 randomized future paths over a forward-looking 2-year window. It uses Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) and Stochastic Volatility to create a dynamic Cone of Uncertainty, modeling how market risk accumulates and changes as time moves forward.

Output & Risk Metrics Glossary

The diagnostic metrics displayed at the bottom of the dashboard are completely dynamic. Rather than displaying static hardcoded values, the backend recalculates these figures in real-time based on your specific historical periods, selected tickers, and cash injections:
• Observed Actual - End: Displays the exact terminal value of your portfolio at the conclusion of the historical backtest window, tracking precisely how your choices would have performed using real historical market data.

• Projected Value - Median: Calculates the P50 median boundary line across all 100 Monte Carlo simulation paths, delivering a balanced, statistically realistic target for the 2-year forward horizon.

• Final Invested Total: Tracks the absolute sum of all your principal capital inputs, adding up every single scheduled purchasing event and recurring contribution you programmed into the timeline.

• Est. Difference Above Contributions: Isolate the net capital appreciation or projected alpha generated by the portfolio, subtracting your raw principal cash injections from the final simulated median wealth profile.

• Estimated Scenario Spread: Highlights the upper and lower confidence intervals of your forecast, explicitly showing you your extreme worst-case market crash exposure (tail-risk) alongside your maximum upside potential.

• Historical Annualized Return: Computes the true Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) your specific portfolio weight allocation achieved during the chosen historical backtest timeframe.
